Our Sacred Journey

Serving Pilgrims Since 1998 in the Holy City

Sam Travels Amritsar began with a solitary ambassador taxi parked outside Amritsar Railway Station, driven by an unyielding vow: every pilgrim stepping foot into the sacred city of Sri Guru Ram Das Ji must be treated like family.

Over 25 years later, our fleet has expanded from individual sedans to luxury Innova Crystas and Tempo Travellers, serving pilgrims arriving from Canada, UK, USA, Australia, and across India for Darshan, Anand Karaj wedding transports, and Wagah Border ceremonies.
